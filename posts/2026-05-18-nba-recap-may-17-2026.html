NBA Nightly Recap — May 17, 2026

Cleveland Cavaliers def. Detroit Pistons

The Cavs didn't just win this one — they suffocated it. Cleveland shot a blistering 51% from the field against a Detroit team that never found its footing offensively, slogging through a 35% / 31% night that turned a competitive matchup into a clinical Cleveland exhibition.

The decisive trio:

- Donovan Mitchell ran the show with a balanced 26-6-8 on 46% shooting, leaning into playmaker mode rather than the volume scoring he's so often forced into. When Mitchell distributes like that — eight dimes in 31 minutes — the Cavs' offense becomes a hydra. - Evan Mobley quietly turned in the best stat line of the night: 21 points, 12 rebounds, 6 assists on 70% shooting. The All-NBA-caliber two-way version of Mobley is the swing factor for everything Cleveland wants to be, and this was that version, screaming through the floor with a 36-minute load. - Sam Merrill was the dagger off the bench — 23 points on 70% shooting in 25 minutes. When a sniper like Merrill is connecting at that clip, Detroit's already-strained defensive rotations get pulled apart at the seams.

Add Jarrett Allen's 23 and 7 on 57%, and Cleveland had four players at 21+ in efficient minutes. That's the kind of depth scoring distribution that wins playoff series.

Key tactical moment: Cleveland's decision to attack early through Mobley as a high-post hub — leveraging his 6 assists to find cutters and shooters — broke Detroit's defensive identity. Once the Pistons started doubling Mobley, Merrill and Mitchell feasted on the closeouts. It's a blueprint Cleveland has been refining all year, and it looked playoff-ready tonight.

For Detroit: Cade Cunningham was held to 13 points on 31% shooting with just 5 assists — a rough night against Cleveland's length and switchable wings. Daniss Jenkins led the team with 17 but needed lots of shots (33% FG) to get there. When your two primary creators combine for 30 points on sub-33% shooting, you're not winning that game against anyone, let alone this Cleveland group.
